WELCOME TO TASIOS ORTHODONTICS!

Our aim is to make a lasting difference in your life, not just through a new smile, but with an unparalleled overall patient experience. We provide state-of-the-art orthodontic treatment in Toronto and Vaughan with an artistic focus in a compassionate, safe and relaxing environment.

Your smile is yours forever, and we want to make it as effortless as possible for you to receive exceptional orthodontic care that fits your busy schedule. At Tasios Orthodontics, you’re well on your way to living your best life with your best smile.

WHAT IS A TASIOS SMILE

A beautiful smile is simply more than straight teeth! The following are features that make an extraordinary Tasios smile. We focus on every detail to maximize the aesthetics of your smile.

Smile Width

A broad, wide smile makes you appear more youthful and provides facial support while you age.
Smile Width

Smile Arc

The edges of your upper front teeth should form an arc that follows the curvature of your lower lip. Flat smiles are considered unaesthetic and associated with aging.
Smile Arc

Smile Details

We focus on every detail from incisor angulation, smile symmetry, tooth shape, and crown parallelism to make your smile perfect.

WHAT MAKES US AMAZING

Voted Top Orthodontic Clinic

For the past 10 years, our community has voted for Tasios Orthodontics as the Top Orthodontic Clinic in Toronto & Vaughan. We’re honored to receive this award, and it reflects our dedication to giving our patients the best possible orthodontic experience. We love serving you!

Convenient Office Hours

We understand your life is busy, so we offer appointment times before and after school as well as on weekends. We value your time and do everything we can to schedule a time that works for you. Choose from our two convenient office locations near major highways in Vaughan and Toronto.

24/7 Emergency Orthodontist

We’re here for you! We provide emergency orthodontist support around the clock to assist our patients with any questions or concerns, including after office hours end. We’re also here to help with emergencies quickly. We aim to make sure your orthodontic experience goes as smoothly as possible.

An Unparalleled Orthodontic Experience

We pay close attention to every detail so that your smile is everything it can be! We are indescribably proud of the results we achieve for our patients, and we invite you to take a look at our transformations page and social media, where we share before and after images!

Amazing Results

Make your smile your best feature with our experts providing you with orthodontics that offers remarkable results. Treatments aren’t just transformative but comfortable with a caring staff taking care of your needs. Get the straight beautiful smile you’ve always dreamed about!

Advanced Technology

Dr. Tasios keeps tabs on the latest technology and uses advanced treatment techniques to minimize discomfort, speed up your treatment and give you the best results. We also use iTero 3D scanners for even greater comfort and a better experience.

Affordable Interest-Free Payment Plans

We’ll work with you to design a personalized interest-free payment plan that fits your budget. Let us know the best monthly payment amount for you, and we’ll set it up. We offer family discounts as well, and we can help you deal with your insurance company to get the most out of your benefits.

Orthodontics for Children

Children should begin visiting an orthodontist by 7 years old. An orthodontist can discover hidden problems with jaw growth, alignment, and emerging teeth – saving your child from future major issues. Our priority is to make them feel comfortable and safe.

Orthodontics For Teens

The most common time for orthodontic treatment. With the influence of social media, your teen may feel more desire than ever to look their best. We can improve their oral health while also giving them a vibrant, beautiful smile that lasts forever.

Orthodontics For Adults

It is never too late to get the beautiful smile that you’ve always wanted. Let us dramatically improve your personal appearance for better self-esteem and confidence. Our modern, discreet options reduce discomfort levels, frequency of visits, and treatment time.
