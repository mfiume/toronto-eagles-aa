Privacy

Film Room, a personal tool for Toronto Eagles U11 AA

What it is

Film Room is a personal tool used by one person to review hockey games. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does with YouTube

It uploads the owner's own game videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, and reads back the status of those videos. It does not access anyone else's account or channel.

What it collects

Nothing from visitors or other users. It does not use cookies, advertising or analytics, and it does not sell or share information with anyone.

Who can see the videos

The owner chooses who can watch each video. Videos are shared only by link with the team's families.
